How engagements work

Nikita Khateev PE contracts directly with clients as an independent business. The terms below are the default; each services agreement can adjust them.

Contracting entity
Nikita Khateev PE, an individual entrepreneur registered in the Republic of Armenia since November 2022 (TIN 40618085). Each engagement is governed by a written services agreement between the client and the PE.
Scope & pricing
Fixed-scope statements of work or time-and-materials retainers. Milestones, deliverables and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing before work starts.
Invoicing
Monthly invoices issued by the PE against delivered milestones or logged hours, paid by bank transfer to the business account. Currency and payment terms are set in the services agreement.
Working model
Fully remote from Yerevan, asynchronous by default. The PE decides where, when and how the work is done, and bears its own equipment, software, infrastructure and business expenses.
Who does the work
All work is performed personally by the lead engineer, Nikita Khateev. The PE has no employees; subcontracting happens only with the client's written agreement.
Independence & IP
The PE is an independent business, not an employee or agent of any client, and may serve several clients concurrently. NDAs and IP assignment on request; open-source deliverables are published under the licence the client chooses.

Nikita Khateev — owner and lead engineer

Nikita is a software engineer who has worked on blockchain systems since 2018, starting with Hyperledger Indy identity infrastructure and moving through EVM protocols to Polkadot runtimes and FHE-enabled chains.
